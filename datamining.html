Formula Reference Sheet

  • Sample Variance: s² = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)
  • Sample Standard Deviation: s = √s²
  • Coefficient of Variation: CV = Std Deviation / Mean
  • Standard Error: SE = σ / √n
  • Sample Covariance: cov(x,y) = Σ(x - x̄)(y - ȳ) / (n - 1)
  • Additive Law: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)
  • Conditional Probability: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
  • Law of Total Probability: P(E) = P(E|H)·P(H) + P(E|not H)·P(not H)
  • Bayes Inference: P(H|E) = P(E|H) · P(H) / P(E)
  • Poisson Distribution: P(y) = (λ^y · e^(−λ)) / y!
  • Binomial Distribution: E(X) = np ; σ² = np(1-p)
