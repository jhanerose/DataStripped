Comprehensive Formula Reference Sheet

  • Probability Formula: P(A) = no. of preferred outcomes / total no. of possible outcomes
  • Independent Events: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
  • Dependent Events: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(A|B)
  • Experimental Probability: p = no. of successful trials / total no. of trials
  • Categorical Outcomes (Expected Frequency): E(A) = P(A) × n
  • Complements: P(A') = 1 - P(A) or P(A) + P(A') = 1
  • Interval Width: Interval width = (largest - smallest) / no. of intervals
  • Mean: x̄ = Σx / n
  • Population Variance: σ² = Σ(x - μ²) / N
  • Sample Variance: s² = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)
  • Standard Deviation: σ = √[Σ(x - μ²) / N] or s = √s²
  • Coefficient of Variation: CV = Standard Deviation / Mean
  • Sample Covariance: cov(x,y) = Σ(x - x̄)(y - ȳ) / (n - 1)
  • Correlation Coefficient (r): rxy = cov(x,y) / (s_x · s_y)
  • Standard Error: SE = s / √n
  • Z-Score: z = (x - μ) / σ
  • Bernoulli Distribution: E(x) = p , σ² = p(1 - p)
  • Binomial Probability Function: P(y) = C(n,y) · p^y · (1-p)^(n-y) where C(n,y) = n! / (y!(n-y)!)
  • Poisson Distribution: P(y) = (λ^y · e^(−λ)) / y! | E(y) = λ, σ² = λ
  • Standardizing Normal Distribution: z = (y - μ) / σ
  • Student's T-Distribution: E(y) = μ (k ≥ 2), σ² = (s² · k) / (k - 2)
  • Chi-Squared Distribution: E(y) = k, σ² = 2k
  • Exponential Distribution: E(y) = 1 / λ, σ² = 1 / λ²
  • Logistic Distribution: E(y) = μ, σ² = (s² · π²) / 3
  • Conditional Probability: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
  • Multiplication Rule: P(A ∩ B) = P(A|B) · P(B)
  • Additive Law: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)
  • Law of Total Probability: P(A) = Σ P(A|B_i) · P(B_i)
  • Bayes Inference: P(H|E) = (P(E|H) · P(H)) / P(E)
